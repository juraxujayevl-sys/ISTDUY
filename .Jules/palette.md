## 2026-03-10 - Modal Accessibility Design Patterns
**Learning:** For multi-step modal dialogs (like reservation systems or wizards) with dense layout elements, establishing standard accessibility configurations (such as explicit label-to-input association using `htmlFor` and `id`, and adding ARIA labels to graphical icons) provides a massive usability upgrade for assistive technologies and improves focus click targets for touch devices without disrupting the existing brand visual aesthetics or style sheets.
**Action:** When implementing modal forms or overlay dialogs in the future, always verify that graphic-only close buttons have explicit descriptive `aria-label` tags and ensure that all text headings designated as "labels" have native `htmlFor` pairings linking them directly to the associated `<input>` or `<select>` controls.
